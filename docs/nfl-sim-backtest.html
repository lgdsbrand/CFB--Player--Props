How to read it

  • Brier and log loss score the home win %: lower is better; 0.25 / 0.693 is a coin flip.
  • Miss is the average gap between the projected and the actual final margin or total, in points.
  • 80% range held should read about 80%.
  • Simulator: every moment played to the end 1,000 times. Each play is a real past NFL play drawn from the same situation (down, distance, field position, clock), tilted toward better plays for the stronger offense; punts, field goals, kickoffs and tries after a touchdown from their own fitted rates. It learns from the four seasons before the test season.
  • Simulator, raw win %: the share of simulated games the home side won, pulled half a game toward even. Simulator then fits it to who actually won on the season before with one scale (the live model's win % is also fitted to wins); the margin and total are the simulated games' own.
  • A: live model: what runs on the site today (the live model backtest's candidate A), fitted on its earlier seasons exactly as in that report.
  • Projection × league share (quarter and half): the pregame projection times the share of points a quarter or half carries, with a normal spread around it. The lines graded are the ones that method would post (its projection rounded to x.5), the way a book sets a line from a number.
  • The pregame projection for every method is the same Massey rating from games before the week.
